FAQ

Questions before you book?

Can UV lashes help with an adhesive allergy?

No. UV lash adhesive still contains cyanoacrylate.

Why can UV curing feel more consistent?

The artist controls when the adhesive cures, so it is less dependent on humidity and temperature.

Are UV lashes completely risk-free?

No professional beauty service is completely risk-free. Proper screening and technique matter.
