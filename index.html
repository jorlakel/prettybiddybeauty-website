The UV Lash Experience

Controlled curing.
Consistent artistry.

UV lash adhesive remains workable until it is activated by a compatible professional light. That gives the artist control over when each bond cures instead of depending only on the room’s humidity and temperature.

The science, made simple

What the testing actually means

The professional light technology used in this service operates in a narrow near-UV/visible-violet range and has been independently evaluated under the IEC 62471 photobiological safety framework. Results depend on device setting, distance, test method, and correct professional use.

403–405nm tested peak

Targeted wavelength range

Published manufacturer testing reports primary output around 403–405 nm, within the compatible curing range for the adhesive system.

IEC62471 evaluation

Independent evaluation

The framework evaluates blue-light, ultraviolet, infrared, retinal, and thermal hazards.

RG1RG2

Risk groups explained

Published results ranged from RG1 to RG2 depending on operating conditions. Professional precautions still matter: eyes stay closed, the beam is positioned correctly, and nobody stares into the light.

UV lash FAQ

Questions before you book?

Are UV lash extensions completely risk-free?

No professional beauty service is completely risk-free. The equipment is used according to professional instructions, eyes remain closed, and direct viewing of the light is avoided.

Can UV lashes help if I am allergic to lash adhesive?

No. UV lash adhesive still contains cyanoacrylate. Clients with a known cyanoacrylate allergy should not book without appropriate medical guidance.

Why can UV curing feel more consistent?

The adhesive remains workable until activated with compatible light, so curing is less dependent on changing room humidity and temperature.
