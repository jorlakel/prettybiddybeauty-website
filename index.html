BEFORE YOU BOOK

Questions? I got you.

How should I prepare?+

Arrive with clean lashes and no eye makeup, mascara, heavy skincare, or oils around your eyes. Avoid caffeine right before your appointment if it makes your eyes flutter.

How long do lashes last?+

Most clients return every two to three weeks for a fill. Retention depends on your natural lash cycle, aftercare, lifestyle, and how quickly your lashes grow.

Can I get UV lashes after an adhesive allergy?+

UV lash adhesive still contains cyanoacrylate, so it cannot guarantee that an allergic reaction will not happen. Contact me before booking so we can discuss your history.

How do I redeem the complimentary UV upgrade?+

Book any new client full set through Booksy. Your complimentary UV lash upgrade will be included automatically, so no coupon code is needed.

What set should I book?+

Choose the style that feels closest to your dream look. I will customize it to your eye shape and natural lashes. When you are between two options, book the fuller set so we have enough time.
